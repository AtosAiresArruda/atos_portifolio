## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, hosted on GitHub Pages (repo `AtosAiresArruda/atos_portifolio`). Chosen by the user so the site stays free to host and easy to edit by hand.

## Users

Recruiters and technical interviewers evaluating Atos Aires Arruda for an **internship in AI / LLM** (fine-tuning, applied vision, automation). They open the link from a CV, LinkedIn or an interview invite, usually on a laptop between calls, sometimes on a phone. They spend under a minute deciding whether to dig deeper, then come back during or after the interview to check specifics.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio that shows, in order of relevance, what Atos has actually built: the LLM fine-tuning work (TCC), computer-vision algorithms running in production at a partner company, research projects, and leadership roles. Success: an interviewer understands within seconds that Atos already trains/adapts models and ships CV code to production, and can reach any detail or contact in one click.

A 6th-semester Computer Science student (UTFPR) whose work is already in production (document stitching + redundancy-discard algorithm, ~10× faster than the linear version) and who is fine-tuning gemma-4-E2B-it with QLoRA for document OCR → JSON, aiming at the same output as large models with far less compute.
